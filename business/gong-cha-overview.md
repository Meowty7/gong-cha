## What is Gong Cha?

Gong Cha (貢茶) is a globally franchised bubble tea (boba) brand originating from Taiwan. It positions itself as a **premium, freshly-brewed whole-leaf tea** chain in contrast to the "mom-and-pop" competitors that use powders or pre-mixes. The brand emphasizes craftsmanship ("tea experts", "real tea, done right") while operating on a fast, scalable QSR (Quick Service Restaurant) model.
